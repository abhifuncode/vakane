Overview

What is ISO 41001?

A standard for running buildings and workplaces efficiently, safely and sustainably.

Who needs it: Facility management companies, real-estate operators and campuses.

Requirements vary with the product, its intended use and where it will be sold. That is why every Vakane Engineering engagement starts with a short scoping call: we confirm whether ISO 41001 applies to you, which standard or scheme governs it, and the most efficient route to approval — before you commit to any testing or fees.

From there, a dedicated specialist manages the entire process — documentation, testing, applications, audits and every question from the issuing body — and keeps you updated weekly until the certificate is in your hands.

FAQ

Common questions about ISO 41001.

Who issues the ISO 41001 certificate?
An accredited, independent certification body. We help you choose one whose accreditation is recognised by your customers.
Can a small business get ISO 41001?
Absolutely. The standard scales to any size. A ten-person business needs far less documentation than a multinational.
Can I combine ISO 41001 with other standards?
Yes. An integrated management system can cover several ISO standards with one set of documents and combined audits.
What is the difference between accredited and non-accredited certificates?
Accredited certificates are issued by bodies supervised by a national accreditation body that is part of the IAF. They are the ones tender committees and global buyers accept.
