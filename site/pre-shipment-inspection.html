Overview

What is Pre-Shipment Inspection?

A random sample check of finished, packed goods against your order before the balance payment is released.

Who needs it: Importers and buyers sourcing from Indian factories.

Requirements vary with the product, its intended use and where it will be sold. That is why every Vakane Engineering engagement starts with a short scoping call: we confirm whether Pre-Shipment Inspection applies to you, which standard or scheme governs it, and the most efficient route to approval — before you commit to any testing or fees.

From there, a dedicated specialist manages the entire process — documentation, testing, applications, audits and every question from the issuing body — and keeps you updated weekly until the certificate is in your hands.

What we check

What we check.

A typical checklist. Your specialist will send a version tailored to your exact product and situation.

Scope and effort depend on

  • Product type
  • Order quantity and AQL level
  • Location of the factory
  • Special tests required
  • Quantity and assortment against the purchase order
  • Workmanship and visual defects (AQL sampling)
  • Dimensions, weight and specifications
  • Functional and safety tests on site
  • Labelling, marking and barcodes
  • Packaging, carton drop test and shipping marks
