Overview

What is Security Certification?

End-to-end support to certify your information security and privacy management, from scoping to audit.

Who needs it: SaaS providers, BPOs, payment companies and data processors.

Requirements vary with the product, its intended use and where it will be sold. That is why every Vakane Engineering engagement starts with a short scoping call: we confirm whether Security Certification applies to you, which standard or scheme governs it, and the most efficient route to approval — before you commit to any testing or fees.

From there, a dedicated specialist manages the entire process — documentation, testing, applications, audits and every question from the issuing body — and keeps you updated weekly until the certificate is in your hands.
