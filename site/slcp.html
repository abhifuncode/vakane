Overview

What is SLCP?

A converged assessment that replaces multiple brand audits with one verified data set.

Who needs it: Apparel and footwear factories.

Requirements vary with the product, its intended use and where it will be sold. That is why every Vakane Engineering engagement starts with a short scoping call: we confirm whether SLCP applies to you, which standard or scheme governs it, and the most efficient route to approval — before you commit to any testing or fees.

From there, a dedicated specialist manages the entire process — documentation, testing, applications, audits and every question from the issuing body — and keeps you updated weekly until the certificate is in your hands.

Documents required

Documents required.

A typical checklist. Your specialist will send a version tailored to your exact product and situation.

Scope and effort depend on

  • The audit protocol requested by your buyer
  • Workforce size
  • Number of buildings or units
  • Previous audit findings
  • Factory licence, fire NOC and statutory registrations
  • Employee records, age proofs and appointment letters
  • Attendance, overtime and wage records
  • PF, ESI and statutory payment challans
  • Health and safety records, drills and first-aid training
  • Policies: HR, grievance, anti-harassment, child labour
  • Environmental permits and waste records
  • Subcontractor and recruitment agency details

FAQ

Common questions about SLCP.

Who appoints the SLCP auditor?
The audit is conducted by an approved third-party audit firm, usually chosen or approved by your buyer. We prepare you; we do not conduct the official audit.
What are the most common findings?
Excessive overtime, incomplete wage records, missing fire safety measures and weak grievance mechanisms top the list.
How often is SLCP repeated?
Typically every 12 to 24 months, depending on your rating and the buyer's programme.
