What auditors look for in an ISO stage 2 audit

Stage 1 checks your paperwork. Stage 2 checks whether people actually follow it. Here is how to prepare.

Most organisations pass the stage 1 document review without trouble. Stage 2 is different: the auditor walks the floor, talks to staff and follows records from start to finish.

Evidence over intent

An auditor is not interested in what you plan to do. They want to see records showing what you have done — at least two to three months of them.

People who know the policy

Expect operators and clerks to be asked what the quality policy means for their job. They don't need to recite it; they need to explain it in their own words.

A completed internal audit and management review

These two activities are non-negotiable before certification. Make sure findings from the internal audit have been acted on and the review minutes show real decisions.

Corrective actions that reach the root cause

When something went wrong, show how you found the underlying reason and changed the system so it won't happen again. That is the heart of every ISO standard.
