Overview

What is Kosher?

Certifies that products comply with Jewish dietary law, a powerful signal in US and European retail.

Who needs it: Food ingredient, spice and additive exporters.

Requirements vary with the product, its intended use and where it will be sold. That is why every Vakane Engineering engagement starts with a short scoping call: we confirm whether Kosher applies to you, which standard or scheme governs it, and the most efficient route to approval — before you commit to any testing or fees.

From there, a dedicated specialist manages the entire process — documentation, testing, applications, audits and every question from the issuing body — and keeps you updated weekly until the certificate is in your hands.

FAQ

Common questions about Kosher.

How is Kosher different from ISO 9001?
ISO 9001 is generic. Kosher adds requirements specific to your industry and is often the scheme buyers in that sector specifically ask for.
Are audits announced?
Some schemes include unannounced audits. We help you stay ready year-round rather than preparing for a date.
Can you help us pick the right scheme?
Yes — that is often the first conversation. We look at your customers, products and markets before recommending a scheme.
