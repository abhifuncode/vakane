Overview

What is Internal Auditor Training?

Practical two-day courses that teach employees to audit their own management system effectively.

Who needs it: Teams maintaining an ISO certified system.

Requirements vary with the product, its intended use and where it will be sold. That is why every Vakane Engineering engagement starts with a short scoping call: we confirm whether Internal Auditor Training applies to you, which standard or scheme governs it, and the most efficient route to approval — before you commit to any testing or fees.

From there, a dedicated specialist manages the entire process — documentation, testing, applications, audits and every question from the issuing body — and keeps you updated weekly until the certificate is in your hands.

FAQ

Common questions about Internal Auditor Training.

Can Internal Auditor Training be delivered in-house?
Yes. In-house sessions are tailored to your processes and are cost-effective for groups of eight or more.
Is there an exam?
Most courses include an assessment. Lead auditor courses include a formal written exam.
Do you offer weekend batches?
Yes, for online and public courses. Ask us for the current schedule.
