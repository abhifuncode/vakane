Certification partners

The right body, the right lab, first time.

Choosing the wrong lab or certification body is the most expensive mistake in certification. Our partner network means you never have to guess.

Accredited certification bodies

We work with IAF-member accredited certification bodies for ISO and sector-scheme audits, and help you choose one whose accreditation your customers recognise.

Recognised testing laboratories

Our lab network includes NABL-accredited and regulator-recognised labs in India, and ILAC-accredited labs overseas for international schemes.

Notified & approved bodies

For CE, UKCA and other third-party schemes we coordinate with notified and approved bodies across Europe and the UK.

Local representatives & agents

Where markets require a local agent, importer of record or authorised representative, we arrange vetted partners.

Inspection & audit networks

Qualified inspectors and auditors across India's manufacturing clusters for inspections, social audits and factory evaluations.

Technology partners

Compliance and GRC platforms that automate evidence collection for security and ESG programmes.
