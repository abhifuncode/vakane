Overview

What is FCC Certification?

Required for electronic devices sold in the US to ensure they don't cause harmful radio interference.

Who needs it: Makers of wireless, IoT and electronic devices for the US.

Requirements vary with the product, its intended use and where it will be sold. That is why every Vakane Engineering engagement starts with a short scoping call: we confirm whether FCC Certification applies to you, which standard or scheme governs it, and the most efficient route to approval — before you commit to any testing or fees.

From there, a dedicated specialist manages the entire process — documentation, testing, applications, audits and every question from the issuing body — and keeps you updated weekly until the certificate is in your hands.

Documents required

Documents required.

A typical checklist. Your specialist will send a version tailored to your exact product and situation.

Scope and effort depend on

  • The product's risk category
  • The directives, regulations or standards that apply
  • Whether a notified or accredited body must be involved
  • How many variants you want covered
  • Product specifications and datasheet
  • Circuit diagrams, assembly drawings or general arrangement drawings
  • Bill of materials and list of critical components with certificates
  • User manual, rating label and warning markings
  • Existing test reports, if any
  • Risk assessment
  • Company registration and manufacturing site details
  • Authorisation for a local representative or importer, where required
Process

How we get you there.

Indicative timeline. Actual timing depends on sample readiness, lab capacity and the issuing body.

Day 1 – 3

Applicability study

We list every regulation and standard that applies, and whether self-declaration or third-party certification is needed.

Week 1

Test plan

Testing

Samples are tested. If something fails, we help your engineers fix it and re-test quickly.

Week 6 – 12

Certification

Ongoing

Surveillance

FAQ

Common questions about FCC Certification.

Can FCC Certification testing be done in India?
Often, yes. Many accredited labs in India are recognised for international schemes. Where testing must happen abroad, we handle sample logistics.
Do I need a local representative?
Some markets require an importer, authorised representative or local agent to be named. We will tell you upfront and can arrange one.
How long does FCC Certification take?
Most projects fall in the range shown above. The biggest variables are sample readiness and how quickly test failures are fixed.
Will one certificate cover all my models?
Models that share the same design, critical components and safety features can usually be grouped. We plan the grouping to minimise cost.
