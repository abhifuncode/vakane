Overview

What is Carbon Footprint?

A defensible calculation of your greenhouse gas emissions following the GHG Protocol or ISO 14064.

Who needs it: Exporters, manufacturers and companies with net-zero commitments.

Requirements vary with the product, its intended use and where it will be sold. That is why every Vakane Engineering engagement starts with a short scoping call: we confirm whether Carbon Footprint applies to you, which standard or scheme governs it, and the most efficient route to approval — before you commit to any testing or fees.

From there, a dedicated specialist manages the entire process — documentation, testing, applications, audits and every question from the issuing body — and keeps you updated weekly until the certificate is in your hands.

FAQ

Common questions about Carbon Footprint.

Do we need software for Carbon Footprint?
Not necessarily. Many first-time reports are done in well-structured spreadsheets. We recommend tools only when the scale justifies them.
Can you arrange third-party assurance?
Yes, we coordinate with independent assurance providers where required.
How often must we report?
Usually annually, though some customers request quarterly data.
